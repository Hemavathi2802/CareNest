# Requirement Analysis

## Purpose
CareNest is a web application for coordinating home healthcare appointments and care between patients, nurses, and administrators.

## User Roles
- **Patient:** creates an account, manages a profile, requests care, and views their care information.
- **Nurse:** manages availability and profile information, reviews assigned appointments, and records care and visit reports.
- **Administrator:** oversees patient and nurse records, appointments, schedules, and visit reports.

## Functional Requirements

### Accounts and Access
1. The system shall allow users to register and sign in.
2. The system shall provide an OTP-based email verification flow during registration and a password-reset flow.
3. Administrator sign-in shall require email OTP verification and provide an administrator session.
4. The system shall provide role-specific patient, nurse, and administrator dashboards.
5. Users shall be able to view and update their profile information.

### Patient Care
6. Patients shall be able to view and update their patient profile.
7. Patients shall be able to request an appointment by selecting a care service, date, and time.
8. Patients shall be able to view appointment status and cancel an appointment.
9. Patients shall be able to view their schedule, medical records, visit reports, and notifications.
10. The system shall send appointment and reminder notifications by email and in-app notification where configured.

### Nurse Operations
11. Nurses shall be able to view their assigned appointments and schedule.
12. Nurses shall be able to accept or reject assigned appointments.
13. Nurses shall be able to view and update their availability and profile.
14. Nurses shall be able to view patients associated with their appointments.
15. Nurses shall be able to record patient-care information and submit visit reports.

### Administration
16. Administrators shall be able to view and manage patient and nurse information.
17. Administrators shall be able to view appointments and manage administrative schedules and nurse availability.
18. Administrators shall be able to review visit reports.

### Data Management
19. The system shall store user, patient, nurse, appointment, availability, schedule, notification, medical-record, patient-care, and visit-report data in a relational database.
20. Appointment patient references shall use the patient profile identifier, not the account user identifier.
21. Medical records shall not store a separate visit-date field; record creation time may be stored independently.

## Non-Functional Requirements
1. **Security and privacy:** authentication and authorization shall ensure that patients, nurses, and administrators can access only permitted operations and records. Patient health information and credentials must not be exposed to unauthorized users. **Current implementation gap:** the backend security configuration currently permits all requests; this requirement is not yet satisfied for a public deployment.
2. **Credential management:** database and email credentials shall be provided through deployment environment variables or a secrets manager, not committed to source control.
3. **Usability:** provide role-appropriate screens and clear feedback for account, appointment, and care workflows.
4. **Reliability:** report failed requests and notification delivery clearly; do not report an operation as successful when it failed.
5. **Maintainability:** keep frontend, backend, and database responsibilities separated and validate changes with automated builds and tests.
6. **Deployment configuration:** the application shall support environment-specific API URLs, CORS origins, database settings, and service ports.

## Scope and Constraints
- The application is a React single-page frontend backed by Spring Boot REST APIs and MySQL.
- Deployment is planned on Railway using separate frontend, backend, and database services.
- Email delivery depends on valid SMTP configuration.
- Performance, availability, backup-retention, and recovery-time targets have not yet been specified.
