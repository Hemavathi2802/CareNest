# Problem Statement

## 1. Project Title
CareNest - Home Healthcare Visit Coordination

## 2. Domain
Home healthcare service coordination: arranging nursing visits at patients' homes and recording care delivered during those visits.

## 3. Stakeholders
- **Patients** need a convenient way to request home-care appointments, see appointment status, and review their care information.
- **Nurses** need a reliable view of assigned appointments, their schedules, and patient information required to document a visit.
- **Administrators** need to manage patient and nurse records, oversee appointments and availability, and review visit reports.

## 4. Problem
Home healthcare visits can be coordinated through phone calls, spreadsheets, and paper records. Disconnected processes make it harder for patients to know the status of a request, for nurses to keep track of assigned visits, and for administrators to maintain consistent patient, nurse, and appointment information. These gaps can contribute to delays, scheduling confusion, and incomplete visit documentation.

The project addresses the coordination and record-keeping problem. It does not diagnose patients, decide treatment, or provide emergency response.

## 5. Proposed Solution
CareNest is a web application through which patients can register, maintain a patient profile, request and track appointments, and view their schedule, notifications, medical records, and visit reports. Nurses can manage profile and availability information, review assigned appointments and schedules, record patient-care information, and submit visit reports. Administrators can manage patient and nurse information and oversee appointments, schedules, availability, and reports.

The application uses distinct user-account and patient-profile identities. Appointment records refer to the patient profile, while the account identity is used to associate a person with their login.

## 6. Project Objectives
- Make home-care appointment requests and status tracking available in one application.
- Give nurses and administrators a shared view of relevant scheduling information.
- Support consistent recording and review of care notes and visit reports.
- Send appointment-related email and in-app notifications when the corresponding services are configured.

## 7. Success Criteria
- A patient can submit a complete appointment request in under one minute under normal operating conditions.
- Patients can find the current status of their appointments without contacting an administrator.
- Nurses can find assigned visits and submit a visit report through the application.
- Administrators can locate patient and nurse information and review appointments and visit reports.

These criteria describe intended outcomes; response-time and conflict-prevention targets require measurement and explicit operational rules.

## 8. Scope
- Patient registration, login, profile, appointment requests, schedules, notifications, medical records, and visit reports.
- Nurse profile and availability, assigned appointments, schedules, patient-care entries, and visit reports.
- Administrative management of patients, nurses, appointments, schedules, availability, and reports.
- Deployment of the frontend, backend, and relational database as separate Railway services.

## 9. Out of Scope
- Emergency response, ambulance dispatch, or urgent triage.
- Automated diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
- Online payment, billing, or insurance claims.
- Video consultation, pharmacy fulfilment, or laboratory booking.
- Integration with external electronic health record systems unless separately specified.

## 10. Constraints and Risks
- Patient medical records and visit reports contain sensitive health information; backend authentication and role-based authorization are required before public deployment.
- The current backend security configuration permits all requests, so the application is not ready for an unrestricted public launch.
- Appointment assignment, conflict handling, care-service duration, cancellation windows, and health-data retention require explicit business rules.
- Email notifications depend on valid SMTP credentials and provider availability.

## 11. Technology Track
Java 21 and Spring Boot backend, React frontend, and MySQL database; deployment is planned on Railway.
